SpotIt: feasibility and scope

Meeting 1 · 14 October 2026

Project scope

All UTP Villages and Academic Blocks are included as configurable locations. Parking capacity and actual parking coordinates require a physical survey before they can be marked verified.

How occupancy works

The current pilot counts active user-reported parking sessions. Each browser account may have one active session; reports expire after four hours and reports older than two hours are flagged as potentially stale. A report is not proof of a physical vehicle.

Automatic detection options (research, not implemented)

  • RFID or NFC gate reads: requires controlled entry and exit, tag registration, reader hardware and reconciliation of missed scans.
  • Camera-based vehicle detection: requires camera placement, permissions, image processing, privacy safeguards and accuracy testing.
  • Per-bay sensors: can indicate bay occupancy but require installation, power and maintenance.
  • GPS/geofencing: can help identify proximity to a parking area, but cannot confirm a vehicle occupies a particular space.

Limitations and future evaluation

Evaluate reporting delay, false reports, forgotten departures, session expiry, network outages and differences between actual and reported occupancy. A production system needs server-side capacity enforcement and authenticated, controlled detector input. Payment and chip integration remain future proposals, not completed features.
