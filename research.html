SpotIt: feasibility and scope

Current SpotIt prototype · UTP

Project overview

SpotIt is a mobile-first parking availability reporting prototype for Universiti Teknologi PETRONAS (UTP). It combines a Leaflet/OpenStreetMap map, Firebase-backed parking reports, GPS proximity checks and administrator-managed parking zones.

Parking zones and availability

Administrators configure parking location names, coordinates, capacity, GPS radius and verification status. Verified zones display estimated Available, Limited or Full conditions; unverified locations are identified separately. Availability is calculated from active parking sessions and unexpired booking holds, not physical sensors.

GPS check-in and confidence

Users report when they park and leave. Check-in checks the browser device's reported location against a configurable zone radius, subject to GPS accuracy. A phone's position does not prove that a vehicle occupies a space. SpotIt marks overdue confirmations as lower-confidence information and supports confirmation reminders and expected departure times.

SpotIt Plus and booking

SpotIt Plus offers nearest-parking search and navigation, ad-free parking details and 15-minute booking holds stored in Firebase. An unexpired booking temporarily reduces reported available spaces; checking in should transition the booking to a parking session without double-counting. Plus access and checkout are prototype interactions, not verified paid subscriptions.

Administration and presentation

Approved Firebase administrators can edit and verify parking zones. Presentation Mode shows sample statuses in the local browser for a project demonstration without modifying actual Firebase sessions, bookings or parking capacities.

Limitations and future work

Availability can differ from real occupancy due to GPS uncertainty, missing reports, forgotten check-outs, connectivity and the absence of physical bay enforcement. Future evaluation could compare reported occupancy with field observations. A production service would need server-enforced booking capacity, subscription verification, reliable expiration cleanup and potentially parking sensors or controlled entry systems.
