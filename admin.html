About SpotIt

A campus parking availability reporting application for Universiti Teknologi PETRONAS (UTP).

How SpotIt works

SpotIt displays reported parking availability for configured UTP parking zones. Verified locations show Available, Limited or Full based on zone capacity, active user parking sessions and unexpired 15-minute bookings. Unverified locations are shown separately.

GPS check-in & reminders

Users can report parking after a GPS proximity check against each zone’s configured radius. GPS shows device proximity, not proof that a car occupies a bay. Parking sessions remain until check-out; overdue confirmations lower data confidence. A reminder asks whether the car is still parked and can record an expected departure time.

Free and SpotIt Plus

Free users can browse reported availability and check in or out. SpotIt Plus adds nearest-parking navigation, 15-minute parking bookings and an ad-free interface. Plus is selectable in this prototype; payment and subscription verification are not connected to a live payment provider.

Administrator tools

Approved administrators manage zone names, coordinates, capacities, GPS radii and verification status. Presentation Mode previews sample parking statuses locally without changing live Firebase parking sessions, bookings or zone capacities.

Accuracy & project limitations

Availability is an estimate derived from crowd-sourced reports and active bookings, not sensor-detected occupancy. GPS accuracy, missing reports and forgotten check-outs can affect reliability. Booking holds do not guarantee a physical bay unless on-site access is enforced.
